MongoDB student benefits, including free MongoDB certification and $50 in MongoDB Atlas credits, are available through the GitHub Student Developer Pack.

To qualify for the GitHub Student Developer Pack, you must meet the following criteria:
  • Be enrolled in a degree- or diploma-granting program.
  • Have a verifiable school-issued email or documentation confirming your student status.
  • Own a GitHub personal account.
  • Be at least 13 years old.

You can find additional instructions for applying to the GitHub Student Developer Pack on GitHub here.

Why wasn't my application approved by GitHub?

Additional troubleshooting resources for GitHub Education access are available here. If you have specific questions regarding the GitHub Student Developer Pack or the status of your GitHub Student Developer Pack application, please contact GitHub support.
Our student benefits, including free certification, are available through the GitHub Student Developer Pack.

To be eligible for free certification, students must:

  1. Be enrolled in the GitHub Student Developer Pack
  2. Verify their student status by entering their GitHub information at mongodb.com/students

Can I reschedule my certification exam?

Yes, test takers may reschedule or cancel a certification exam up to 24 hrs before the scheduled test time without penalty. To make changes, visit your learner dashboard on MongoDB University.

I missed my scheduled certification exam. Can I request another free certification voucher code?

MongoDB for Students does not offer additional free certification vouchers to students who miss their scheduled certification exam.

Can I take more than one certification exam?

Yes, students are eligible for free certification vouchers for each related learning path they complete. To unlock additional certification benefits, you must finish the learning path associated with your first voucher before claiming another.

Are there any resources to help me prepare for the certification exam?

To prepare for your certification exam, you can access free exam preparation materials available on MongoDB University, including:
  • Study Guides
  • Practice Questions
Additionally, check out the Certification Success for Students guide, specifically created to support students preparing for their MongoDB certification exam.

What is the passing criteria for the certification exam?

MongoDB certification exams have a pass/fail grading system, with the required passing percentage varying by exam. Candidates must meet the overall passing score, not individual domain scores, and domain weightings are detailed in the Exam Study Guide. After the exam, candidates receive a score report showing their result and areas for improvement.

For more details see the Certification Program Guide.

Why should I earn a MongoDB Certification?

Earning a MongoDB Certification validates your skills and knowledge, helping you stand out in the tech industry. MongoDB Certified Professionals are hired by organizations worldwide, making this certification a valuable addition to your resume. It boosts your credibility, keeps you competitive, and enhances your employability in today's job market.
We recommend beginning with the MongoDB Basics for Students Skill Badge. It's an interactive way to learn MongoDB essentials while earning a Credly badge for your resume or LinkedIn profile. After completing this badge, explore additional MongoDB Skill Badges or pursue a MongoDB Certification.

How do Atlas promotional codes work?

MongoDB Atlas is billed hourly based on usage. As part of the GitHub Student Developer Pack, we provide $50 in Atlas credits to apply to your Atlas organization. To redeem this, you'll need a valid credit card or a PayPal account linked to your Atlas account.

How do I apply the Atlas promotional code to claim free credits?

Once you've received your Atlas promotional code, you can apply the credits to the selected Atlas organization in your account. For detailed instructions on how to apply a promo code, visit this link. Please note, promotional codes are redeemed at the organization level and each promotional code can only be redeemed once to an organization of your choice.

Do the Atlas promotional codes expire?

Yes, Atlas promotional codes will expire if they are not applied to your account within 90 days. We recommend generating an Atlas code only when you are ready to apply it to your Atlas organization to avoid expiration.
