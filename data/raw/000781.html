Why the best AI apps are built on MongoDB

MongoDB has all the flexibility, performance, and security developers need to create transformative AI-powered experiences for a new generation of modern applications.

Fluent Across Data Types

Stop fighting your schema. The document model matches your code, not the other way around. Handle any data type at scale—whether it's text, event streams, or vector representations of text, video, or audio for next-gen semantic search.

Distributed Architecture

Run AI workloads without slowing down your core operational database. MongoDB's modern architecture lets you isolate and scale independently. You get optimized performance for even the most demanding, unpredictable workloads.

Rapid Innovation

What happens when you give developers flexible tools? They move faster. MongoDB gives your teams the power to ship AI applications and deploy new features quickly. Don’t allow traditional data models to slow your growth.

Run Anywhere

MongoDB allows you to run anywhere—on your laptop, in your data center, across clouds, or in hybrid environments—to easily meet low-latency performance and data sovereignty requirements with no vendor lock-in.

Simplify Complexity

MongoDB delivers every capability you need for modern AI apps in one unified platform: text search, vector search, best-in-class embedding models from Voyage AI, and real-time operational queries. Stop managing a dozen tools.

Robust Security

Security and data protection are at the core of MongoDB. Industry-first queryable encryption protects data at rest where it’s stored, in motion over networks, and even while being processed to meet the most stringent regulatory and compliance requirements.
